🏋️ Bars

Uneven bars & horizontal bar. Build swinging power, body tension, and release skills from intermediate to elite.

Intermediate

Intermediate Skills

⚡

Kip

Also known as: Glide kip, mount kip

Transitions from below the bar to front support in one fluid motion. The first major competitive bars skill.

Before you try this
  • Solid glide swing with hollow/arch rhythm
  • Front support hold 10 seconds
  • 10 controlled pull-ups
Safety Tips
  • Always use a spotter for first 20+ attempts
  • Practice on a low bar with a mat underneath
  • Timing matters more than strength — don't muscle it
  1. Start with a glide swing — push feet forward on the forward swing.
  2. As you swing forward, bring toes up to the bar.
  3. At the moment feet touch the bar, shoot hips up explosively.
  4. Simultaneously push the bar toward your hips and lean forward.
  5. Finish in a strong front support with straight arms and tight core.
🚀

Cast to Horizontal

Also known as: Cast, cast to handstand (full version)

From front support, swing legs away from the bar to horizontal. Foundation for giants and release moves.

Before you try this
  • Consistent kip to front support
  • Hollow body hold 30 seconds
Safety Tips
  • Spotter supports lower back as hips rise
  • Never cast past horizontal until you have full control
  • Return to front support with control — absorb the swing
  1. Start in a front support, arms straight, shoulders over bar.
  2. Swing legs back and down, then push hips up by pressing the bar.
  3. Keep core tight, legs together, toes pointed throughout.
  4. Reach horizontal — body flat — as the first goal.
  5. Return to front support with control, absorbing the swing.
🔄

Back Hip Circle

Also known as: BHC, hip circle

A full backward rotation around the bar with hips pressed to the bar throughout.

Before you try this
  • Solid cast to horizontal
  • Front support hold 10 seconds
Safety Tips
  • Spotter stands to the side, hand on back to guide rotation
  • Keep hips glued to bar the whole time — gaps cause stalls
  • Use a low bar first with thick mat underneath
  1. From front support, cast slightly to get momentum.
  2. As you come back, tip body backward, hips pressed firmly on bar.
  3. Circle backward — hips stay touching the bar at all times.
  4. Use core to scoop and close at the bottom to complete rotation.
  5. Finish in a front support with chest up and arms straight.
Advanced

Advanced Skills

🌀

Giant Swing

Also known as: Giant, full giant, clear circle

A full 360° rotation around the bar in a straight body position. The backbone of all advanced bar routines.

Before you try this
  • Cast to handstand (full)
  • Strong back hip circle
  • Good shoulder flexibility
  • Bar grips strongly recommended
Safety Tips
  • Coach must spot — no exceptions for first giants
  • Use a pit bar or foam pit for initial attempts
  • Never attempt without proper wrist and grip preparation
  • Stop immediately if shoulders feel any strain
  1. Cast to handstand — must reach at least 90% of the way up.
  2. At the top, keep arms straight and core tight in a slight hollow.
  3. As you descend on the back side, keep body long and shift to arch.
  4. Hit the bottom with maximum swing — don't pike or break at hips.
  5. As you rise on the front side, shift back to hollow and press bar down.
  6. Finish back at handstand with straight arms. Repeat for multiples.
🦅

Tkatchev (Release Move)

Also known as: Reverse hecht, counter Tkatchev

A release move where the gymnast lets go of the bar, flies backward over it, and re-catches. For experienced gymnasts only.

Before you try this
  • Consistent giants (10+ in a row)
  • Strong cast-to-handstand
  • Proficient on strap bar release drills
  • Coach approval required
Safety Tips
  • Foam pit only for first attempts — no exceptions
  • Trained coach must spot every attempt
  • Perfect giants required before starting this progression
  • Learn on strap bar before full bar
  1. Build several consecutive giants to get maximum swing.
  2. On the back swing coming down from handstand, keep body long.
  3. As you rise on the front side, open shoulders aggressively and push bar away.
  4. Release and fly backward in straddle or pike over the bar.
  5. Reach back and re-catch the bar with straight arms.
  6. Re-catch into a giant swing continuation.
