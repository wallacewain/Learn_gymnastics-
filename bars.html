🏋️ Bars

Uneven bars & horizontal bar. Build grip strength, body tension, and swinging skills from the ground up.

Beginner

Foundation Skills

✋

Dead Hang

Also known as: Passive hang

The starting point for all bar work. Builds grip strength and teaches body awareness on the bar.

Before you try this
  • No prerequisites — this is the very first skill
Safety Tips
  • Use chalk or grips if hands are slipping
  • Keep shoulders active — don't fully relax them
  • Stand on a mat that lets you dismount safely
  1. Stand under the bar and grip it with both hands, shoulder-width apart, overhand grip.
  2. Bend knees and lift feet off the ground slowly.
  3. Keep arms straight. Pull shoulders slightly down and back — don't shrug.
  4. Hold for 5–10 seconds. Build up to 30 seconds over time.
  5. Lower feet to the mat gently — never drop.
💪

Chin-Up / Pull-Up

Also known as: Bar pull-up

Essential upper body strength needed for every bars skill. Chin-ups use underhand grip; pull-ups use overhand.

Before you try this
  • Comfortable dead hang for 15+ seconds
Safety Tips
  • Don't kip or kick to get up — use slow, controlled strength
  • Never fully lock out elbows at the bottom with force
  • Have a spotter nearby when starting out
  1. Start in a dead hang with overhand grip.
  2. Engage your core — no swinging.
  3. Pull elbows down toward your hips as you lift your chin above the bar.
  4. Hold briefly at the top, then lower slowly (3–4 seconds down).
  5. Aim for 3 clean reps before moving to the next skill.
🌊

Glide Swing

Also known as: Glide kip prep

A swinging motion that teaches the hollow-arch body shape used in all bar swings.

Before you try this
  • 5 pull-ups or chin-ups
  • Strong hollow body hold on the floor (10+ seconds)
Safety Tips
  • Always have a coach spot your first swings
  • Land on a thick mat if you let go
  • Keep grip tight — thumbs wrapped around the bar
  1. Jump to hang on a low bar with overhand grip.
  2. Swing forward: push hips forward and make a hollow body (ribs in, core tight).
  3. As you swing back, make an arch body (chest open, legs behind).
  4. Alternate hollow on the way forward, arch on the way back.
  5. Practice until the rhythm feels natural with no extra kicks.
🙌

Front Support

Also known as: Support hold

Holding your body above the bar with straight arms. The basis for casts, giants, and dismounts.

Before you try this
  • 5 pull-ups with good form
  • Strong plank hold (30+ seconds)
Safety Tips
  • Have a spotter hold your hips the first few times
  • If your arms shake, you need more strength — come down
  • Dismount by stepping back to mat, never forward off the bar
  1. From a pull-up, continue lifting until your hips reach bar height.
  2. Push the bar down and lean chest slightly forward over the bar.
  3. Straighten arms fully. Squeeze legs together and point toes.
  4. Hold for 3 seconds. Build to 10 seconds over time.
  5. Lower back down with control — don't just drop.
Intermediate

Building Skills

⚡

Kip

Also known as: Glide kip, mount kip

The kip is often the first competitive skill. It transitions from below the bar to a front support in one fluid motion.

Before you try this
  • Solid glide swing with good hollow/arch
  • Front support hold 10 seconds
  • 10 pull-ups
Safety Tips
  • Always use a spotter for first 20+ attempts
  • Practice on a low bar with a mat underneath
  • Don't muscle it — timing matters more than strength
  1. Start with a glide swing — push feet forward during the forward swing.
  2. As you swing forward and your feet go out in front, bring toes to the bar.
  3. At the moment your feet touch the bar, shoot your hips up explosively.
  4. Simultaneously push the bar toward your hips and lean forward.
  5. Finish in a strong front support with arms straight and core tight.
🚀

Cast

Also known as: Cast to handstand (advanced version)

From front support, swing your legs up and away from the bar. The foundation for giants and release moves.

Before you try this
  • Consistent kip
  • Comfortable front support hold
  • Hollow body hold (30 seconds)
Safety Tips
  • Spotter should support lower back as hips rise
  • Never cast past horizontal until you have perfect control
  • Land back to front support — don't pike and drop
  1. Start in a front support, arms straight, shoulders over bar.
  2. Swing legs back and down, then push hips up by pressing the bar.
  3. Keep core tight, legs together, toes pointed throughout.
  4. Reach horizontal (body flat) as the first goal.
  5. Return to front support with control — absorb the swing, don't slam down.
🔄

Back Hip Circle

Also known as: BHC, hip circle

A full rotation backward around the bar while keeping hips close. A classic beginner-competitive skill.

Before you try this
  • Solid cast to horizontal
  • Front support hold 10 seconds
  • Comfortable swinging on bars
Safety Tips
  • Spotter stands to the side with hand on your back to guide rotation
  • Keep hips glued to bar the whole time — gaps cause stalls
  • Use a low bar first, thick mat underneath
  1. From front support, cast slightly to get momentum.
  2. As you come back, tip body backward and keep hips pressed on bar.
  3. Circle backward — hips stay touching bar at all times.
  4. Use core to scoop and "close" at the bottom to come around.
  5. Finish in a front support with chest up and arms straight.
Advanced

Advanced Skills

🌀

Giant Swing

Also known as: Giant, full giant

A full 360° rotation around the bar in a straight body position. The backbone of advanced bar routines.

Before you try this
  • Cast to handstand (full)
  • Strong back hip circle
  • Good shoulder flexibility
  • Bar grips recommended
Safety Tips
  • Coach must spot — always. No exceptions for first giants.
  • Use a pit bar or foam pit for initial attempts
  • Never attempt without proper wrist/grip preparation
  • Stop immediately if shoulders feel any strain
  1. Cast to handstand — must be at least 90% of the way up.
  2. At the top, keep arms straight and core tight. Slight hollow.
  3. As you come down on the back side, keep body long and shift to arch.
  4. Hit the bottom with maximum swing — don't pike or break at hips.
  5. As you rise on the front side, shift back to hollow and press bar down.
  6. Finish back at handstand with straight arms. Repeat for multiple giants.
🦅

Tkatchev (Release Move)

Also known as: Reverse hecht, toe-on Tkatchev

A release move where the gymnast lets go of the bar, flies backward over it, and re-catches. For experienced gymnasts only.

Before you try this
  • Consistent giants (10+ in a row)
  • Strong cast-to-handstand
  • Proficient in multiple release moves on straps
  • Coach approval required
Safety Tips
  • Only attempt in foam pit first — no exceptions
  • Trained coach must spot every attempt
  • Must have perfect giants before starting this progression
  • Learn on strap bar or low bar before full bar
  1. Build several consecutive giants to get maximum swing.
  2. On the back swing (coming down from handstand), keep body long.
  3. As you rise on the front side, open shoulders aggressively and push bar away.
  4. Release the bar and fly backward in a straddled or piked position over the bar.
  5. Reach back and re-catch the bar with straight arms.
  6. Re-catch into a giant swing continuation.
