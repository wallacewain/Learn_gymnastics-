💫 Floor

Tumbling, leaps, and artistry. Intermediate to advanced floor skills — built on solid technique and safe progressions.

Intermediate

Intermediate Skills

🌊

Round-Off

Also known as: Hurdle round-off

A cartwheel that lands facing backward on two feet. The most important tumbling skill — connects running to back tumbling.

Before you try this
  • Strong cartwheel in both directions
  • Good straight body jump
Safety Tips
  • Learn on a soft mat with a spotter
  • Don't pike down at the top — stay hollow
  • Land two feet simultaneously — not one at a time
  1. Run 3–4 steps, hurdle onto one foot.
  2. Kick into a cartwheel but place both hands together on the mat.
  3. As legs come over, snap them down together — closing the body.
  4. Push off mat with both hands to generate backward rebound.
  5. Land two-footed facing the direction you came from, arms up.
💥

Back Handspring

Also known as: BHS, flic-flac, flip-flop

The cornerstone of back tumbling. Once consistent, every advanced skill becomes accessible.

Before you try this
  • Strong round-off
  • Comfortable back walkover
  • Spotter-assisted standing BHS with good form
Safety Tips
  • Always spotted for first 50+ attempts — non-negotiable
  • Use an overhead harness rig in a proper gym
  • Never kick up — jump back and then arch
  • Wrists must be properly conditioned before starting
  1. Stand with arms by sides, bend knees slightly (dip).
  2. Swing arms and jump backward and upward — not straight back.
  3. Arch body as you leave ground, arms reaching overhead.
  4. Hands contact mat simultaneously — push fast.
  5. Snap feet over quickly, landing with bent knees and arms up.
🌪️

Back Tuck Salto

Also known as: Back tuck, tuck back, back salto

A backward somersault in tuck position. Often the first salto gymnasts learn — the gateway to advanced tumbling.

Before you try this
  • Consistent back handspring
  • Round-off back handspring with power
  • Tuck jump with good height
  • Harness or pit training with coach sign-off
Safety Tips
  • Foam pit only for first attempts — always
  • Overhead harness rig strongly recommended
  • Trained coach must spot for every early attempt
  • Do not attempt if mentally blocked — fear causes injury
  1. From round-off BHS, punch off two feet — jumping straight UP.
  2. At peak height, pull knees tight to chest and grab shins.
  3. Tuck chin slightly — don't throw your head back.
  4. Spot the floor as you rotate — open when you see the mat.
  5. Land two-footed with bent knees. Stick!

Advanced Skills

Back Layout Salto

Also known as: Layout, back layout, whip back

A backward salto in a straight (layout) body position. Requires significantly more height and power than a back tuck.

Before you try this
  • Consistent round-off BHS back tuck
  • Good body tension and hollow position
  • Harness and pit training mandatory
Safety Tips
  • Foam pit required for early attempts — no exceptions
  • Overhead harness training is the standard path
  • Never rush from tuck to layout — respect the progression
  1. Run, round-off, back handspring — building maximum power.
  2. Punch off two feet, driving arms up aggressively.
  3. Keep body long and tight — do NOT tuck.
  4. Head neutral, arms by sides or overhead.
  5. Rotate in a tight hollow body position.
  6. Open eyes, spot the floor, land with bent knees.
🌟

Full Twist (Layout Full)

Also known as: Full, layout full, 1/1 twist

A layout salto with a 360° twist. One of the most iconic and beautiful skills in gymnastics.

Before you try this
  • Consistent round-off BHS layout
  • Half-twist in pit — comfortable
  • Strong aerial body tension
  • Coach and program director approval
Safety Tips
  • Foam pit and overhead harness required for all initial attempts
  • Initiate twist from the shoulders — don't throw your head
  • Never attempt a full twist when fatigued
  1. Execute a powerful round-off BHS into the punch.
  2. Jump and initiate layout rotation — body stays long.
  3. At the top of the flip, pull one arm across body to start the twist.
  4. Keep body tight in layout throughout the twist.
  5. Spot the floor as you complete the twist.
  6. Open arms to slow rotation, land two-footed and stick.
