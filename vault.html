🏃 Vault

Power, speed, and precision. Master the run, hurdle, and block before any vault skill.

Beginner

Foundation Skills

🏃

Sprint Run Approach

Also known as: Vault run, approach run

The vault starts with a powerful sprint. A consistent, fast run is the foundation of every vault.

Before you try this
  • No prerequisites — this is the starting point
Safety Tips
  • Always wear gymnastics or athletic shoes on the runway
  • Clear the runway — no equipment or people in the path
  • Mark a consistent starting spot so every run is the same
  1. Mark your starting position (measured from the vault table).
  2. Begin with a few accelerating steps, building to a full sprint.
  3. Run on the balls of your feet — not heel to toe.
  4. Stay low slightly until the last 3 steps, then rise and drive into hurdle.
  5. Drill this 10+ times every practice, even without vaulting.
🐰

Hurdle onto Springboard

Also known as: Hurdle, jump to board

The transition from run to jump. A good hurdle converts horizontal speed into vertical power.

Before you try this
  • Consistent sprint approach with proper form
Safety Tips
  • Springboard must be secured/placed on non-slip surface
  • Land in center of board — not on the edge
  • Practice hurdle onto a mat before using the board
  1. From the run, take a one-foot takeoff step (hurdle step).
  2. Drive that knee up as the other leg sweeps behind.
  3. Land two feet simultaneously in the center of the springboard.
  4. Feet should be shoulder-width and slightly in front of your hips.
  5. Immediately explode upward off the board.
🙌

Straight Jump Over Vault (Squat On)

Also known as: Squat-on dismount, straddle over

The most basic vault — jumping onto the vault table and off again. Teaches the block and landing.

Before you try this
  • Consistent hurdle to two-foot landing
  • Comfortable jump over obstacle on floor
Safety Tips
  • Thick landing mat on the far side of vault — always
  • Spotter stands on far side to catch if needed
  • Vault table at lowest setting for beginners
  1. Run, hurdle onto board, and jump with arms reaching to vault table.
  2. Place both hands on vault table simultaneously as feet come up.
  3. In squat-on: bring both feet up between your hands and land on vault.
  4. Stand up briefly, then jump off with a stuck two-foot landing.
  5. Bend knees on landing, arms forward — stick it!
Intermediate

Building Skills

⚡

Handspring Vault (Yurchenko Entry)

Also known as: Front handspring vault, FHS

A true vaulting skill — the gymnast goes over the table in a front handspring motion, landing on feet.

Before you try this
  • Front handspring on floor — automatic, no thinking
  • Squat-on and straddle-over vault mastered
  • Strong, fast run and solid hurdle
  • Powerful punch off springboard
Safety Tips
  • Use a vault trainer (raised mat stacked) for first attempts
  • Coach spots from the far side of the table
  • Thick landing mat is non-negotiable
  • Never attempt without a qualified spotter
  1. Build a fast, consistent run and powerful hurdle.
  2. From the board, push off with straight body and arms reaching to vault.
  3. Hands contact vault table simultaneously — push HARD and FAST.
  4. The block (push) happens in under 0.1 seconds — practice on a wall first.
  5. Body rises over the table, hips over shoulders.
  6. Open and land with bent knees, arms forward, feet together.
Advanced

Advanced Skills

🌪️

Yurchenko Vault

Also known as: Round-off entry vault, Yurchenko family

The Yurchenko uses a round-off onto the board and back handspring onto the table, adding post-flight twists or saltos.

Before you try this
  • Excellent round-off back handspring on floor
  • Front handspring vault with good form
  • Round-off entry onto board (drill phase)
  • Mandatory coach assessment and approval
Safety Tips
  • Begin with Yurchenko drills on a stacked mat — not the vault
  • Pit landing required for all first attempts
  • Two spotters recommended for initial full Yurchenkos
  • Never rush this progression — wrists and shoulders are at risk
  1. Drill: round-off onto board, back handspring onto a high stacked mat.
  2. Run, hurdle, round-off with feet landing together on the board.
  3. Immediately rebound into a back handspring motion onto vault table.
  4. Hands contact table and block explosively.
  5. Post-flight: tuck, pike, or twist depending on vault type.
  6. Land on thick mat, feet together, knees bent.
🦸

Tsukahara Vault

Also known as: Tsuk, half-on entry vault

A half-turn onto the vault with a back salto off. A powerful, technical vault for advanced gymnasts.

Before you try this
  • Front handspring vault — excellent form
  • Half-turn onto vault drills mastered
  • Back tuck on floor — automatic
  • Coach and program director sign-off
Safety Tips
  • Foam pit required for all early attempts
  • Spotter must be in place every attempt until landing is consistent
  • Wrist conditioning program required before starting
  1. Run and hurdle as normal.
  2. Take off from board sideways, one hand contacts vault first.
  3. Rotate half turn as body rises over the table.
  4. Push off vault with both hands in a block motion.
  5. Back salto (tuck, pike, or layout) in post-flight.
  6. Land two-footed with solid stick.
