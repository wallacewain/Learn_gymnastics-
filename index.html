Safety First — Always

Gymnastics is an incredible sport, but every skill must be earned safely. Follow these rules every single time.

👩‍🏫

Train With a Coach

Never attempt new skills alone. A qualified coach can spot you, correct technique, and prevent injury.

🏋️

Warm Up Properly

Spend at least 15 minutes warming up muscles and joints before any gymnastics practice. Cold muscles tear.

🛏️

Use Proper Mats

Always use crash mats, landing mats, or foam pits when learning new skills. Never skip matting.

📈

Master Progressions

Every advanced skill has a beginner step. Never skip levels — progressions exist to protect you.

🩺

Listen to Your Body

Pain is a stop signal, not a challenge. Rest when hurt and see a doctor for any persistent pain.

🔁

Repetition Builds Safety

Drill basics until they are automatic. Consistent, correct repetition is how gymnasts stay safe.
